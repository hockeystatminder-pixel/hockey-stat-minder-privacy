Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Short version: Hockey Stat Minder stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Information We Collect

Hockey Stat Minder does not collect any personal information. All data you enter — including team names, player names, jersey numbers, and game statistics — is stored locally on your device using Apple's standard storage APIs.

No data is transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Data Storage

All app data is stored exclusively on your device. If you delete the app, all data associated with it is permanently removed. We have no ability to recover or access your data.

Analytics and Tracking

Hockey Stat Minder does not use any analytics services, crash reporting tools, or tracking technologies. We do not track how you use the app.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party services that collect data. The optional feedback button opens your device's Mail app to send an email directly — no data passes through our servers.

Children's Privacy

Hockey Stat Minder does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data leaves your device, the app is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will revise the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at hockeystatminder@gmail.com.
